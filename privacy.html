Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

SE Budget ("the app") helps self-employed people track income, business costs and home costs. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

Who this covers

SE Budget is developed and operated by an individual developer, contactable at arifdub@gmail.com. That's who you reach for any question about this policy or your data.

If you don't create an account

SE Budget works fully without signing in. In that case, everything you enter — your entries, categories, recurring rules and targets — is stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. It never reaches us, and we have no way to see it.

If you create an account

Signing in (with an email and password, or with Google) lets your data sync across your own devices. Creating an account sends the following to our database provider, Supabase, associated with your account:

  • Your email address — used only to identify your account and, if you ask, to reply to feedback you send.
  • Your entries — the type (income, business cost or home cost), category, amount, payment method and date of each entry you add.
  • Your settings — daily/weekly/monthly targets, any custom categories you create, and any recurring entry rules you set up.

This data exists to make the app work for you — showing your own totals and reports, and keeping your own entries in sync between your own devices. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to profile you. The legal basis for this processing is the contract formed by you creating an account and asking us to provide this service; it's kept for as long as your account exists, and removed as described under "Deleting your data" below.

Feedback messages

If you use the "Send feedback" button in Settings, your message — and, only if you choose to include one, a reply-to email address — is sent directly to the developer's inbox via Resend, an email delivery service. It is not stored in the app's database and is not linked to your account.

Analytics and visit counter (optional, off by default)

The app asks, on first visit, whether you're willing to allow basic analytics so the developer can see roughly how many people use the app and which pages they land on. Nothing in this section loads or records anything until you tap "Allow" — declining (or simply not answering) means none of it runs. This single choice covers two things:

  • Google Analytics — only loads if you allow it. IP addresses are anonymized, and no entry data, category names, or amounts are ever included — Google only ever sees that a visit happened, not what you did in the app.
  • A basic visit counter — records the page path, a random id your browser generates for itself and stores locally, and the time, so the developer can see how many people are using the site and which pages they land on. This isn't linked to your account, name or email, and sends nothing to Google or any other third party — it's stored only in our own database.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's browser storage (or your browser's site data for sebudget.com), which resets the choice and asks again on your next visit.

Who else sees this data

  • Supabase — hosts the account sign-in system and the database that stores your entries and settings, if you're signed in, and the anonymous visit counter described above.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with a Google account, or if you allow the optional analytics described above.
  • Resend — only used to deliver a feedback message you choose to send, as described above.

Your entries and settings are never sold, rented, or shared with any other party, and are never used for advertising.

Keeping your data to yourself

The database enforces, at the database level, that a signed-in account can only ever read or write its own entries and settings — not any other user's, regardless of what the app itself does.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any single entry any time, directly in the app.
  • Delete every entry at once with "Delete all entries" in Settings → Danger zone (your targets and theme are kept).
  • Delete your account and every trace of it — your email address and all synced entries and settings — immediately and yourself, with "Delete account…" in Settings → Account → Manage account. No waiting period: this removes it straight away.
  • If you'd rather ask us to do it for you, or the button isn't working, email arifdub@gmail.com — handled within 30 days at the very most, usually much sooner.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us at any time to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it, if anything is wrong (you can also just edit most of it yourself in the app).
  • Erase it — see "Deleting your data" above for the self-serve way to do this immediately.
  • Restrict or object to how it's processed.
  • Receive a copy of your entries in a portable format (CSV export is already built into Reports; ask if you'd like your full account data in another format).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics and the visit counter at any time — see above.

To exercise any of these, email arifdub@gmail.com. If you're ever unhappy with how a request was handled, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority — in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission.

Children's privacy

SE Budget is a business-finance tool for self-employed adults and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: arifdub@gmail.com.
