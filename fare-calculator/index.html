How it's worked out: the first 500m is the initial charge. The next 15km (Standard/Premium) is charged per kilometre up to a capped amount, since the meter actually runs on whichever of distance or time is ticking up faster — this calculator estimates using the per-km rate only. Beyond that, every further kilometre is charged at the higher "Tariff B" rate, with no cap. The Special rate (late night weekends, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve) has no capped middle tariff — everything past the initial charge is charged at its higher per-km rate. Pre-booking adds a flat €3.00, and the first passenger is included free — each extra passenger adds the fee above (edit it if yours differs). This is an estimate only, not an official fare — the actual taximeter reading is what's payable.
