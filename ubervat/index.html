Example: €10,000 in earnings × 12% commission = €1,200 commission. VAT at 23% on that €1,200 = €276.

Uber charges this commission without Irish VAT, since Uber B.V. is based in the Netherlands — most self-employed Irish drivers must self-account for this VAT under the reverse charge mechanism, rather than Uber adding it to an invoice. This calculator just does the arithmetic; it isn't tax advice — check with Revenue or an accountant for how it applies to your own situation.
